
# Privacy Policy — Obsidian Rclone

Last updated: November 17, 2026

## 1. Overview

Obsidian Rclone is a personal-use file synchronization workflow operated by its owner.

It uses rclone and Google Drive API to maintain a backup mirror of personal Obsidian documents.

## 2. Data Access

The application requests Google Drive access to list, upload, update, and delete files.

Although Google OAuth grants broader Drive access, the configured synchronization job targets designated document and backup folders.

## 3. Data Usage

Google Drive access is used for file synchronization, backup maintenance, and verification.

Files are transferred from Alibaba Cloud OSS to Google Drive through a privately operated server.

## 4. Data Storage

OAuth credentials are stored on the private server.

Document copies and historical backups are stored in Google Drive.

Execution logs may be stored on the server for troubleshooting.

## 5. Data Sharing

The synchronization workflow does not sell user data or intentionally share it with advertising services.

Google Drive data may be accessed through separately authorized services, subject to their own permissions and privacy policies.

## 6. Security

The operator is responsible for protecting OAuth credentials, server access, and cloud storage permissions.

## 7. Data Deletion

Files deleted from the source are removed from the active Google Drive mirror during synchronization.

Historical copies may remain in a separate backup folder until explicitly deleted.

## 8. Changes

This policy may be updated if the synchronization workflow or data handling practices change.

## 9. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application operator through the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
